### AdsAiPro

AdsAiPro is an AI-powered decision workspace for advertising and operations teams. It brings together advertising spend, member behavior, and affiliate referral data to help teams assess channel quality, track how much of their investment they recover, and decide where to allocate budget or focus follow-up.

AdsAiPro 是一套面向广告投放与运营团队的 AI 经营决策工作台。它整合广告消耗、会员行为和代理推广数据，帮助团队评估不同渠道的获客质量、追踪投放成本回收，并为预算分配和运营跟进提供依据。

- **Acquisition and channel performance｜获客与渠道表现**  
  Compare spend, conversion from impressions to sign-ups and first deposits, retention, and ROI across advertisers and subchannels. Drill down from dashboard metrics to the underlying records.  
  按投放商和子渠道比较广告消耗、曝光到注册和首充的转化、留存与 ROI，并从经营指标下钻至数据明细。
- **Member and affiliate insights｜会员与代理洞察**  
  Assess member activity, value, and risk indicators, and track the growth and retention of members referred by each affiliate.  
  分析会员活跃度、价值与风险指标，追踪各代理带来的会员增长与留存表现。
- **Member value forecasting and AI interpretation｜会员价值预测与 AI 解读**  
  Use early activity after a member’s first deposit to forecast cumulative value over the 30 days beginning on the first deposit date. AI interprets changes in calculated metrics and suggests where to investigate or follow up.  
  利用首充后初期的行为数据，预测会员自首充当天起 30 天的累计价值（pLTV）。AI 基于已计算的指标解读变化，并提出后续核查与运营建议。
- **Data ingestion and synchronization｜数据接入与同步**  
  Import daily ad reports, member records, and daily activity data, synchronize reports from Google Sheets, and monitor import progress and data gaps.  
  接入广告日报、会员档案和每日行为数据，同步 Google Sheets 报表，并追踪导入进度与数据缺口。
- **Scenario modeling and payback analysis｜情景测算与回本分析**  
  Model how budget, retention, and payment assumptions affect projected cash flow and payback periods, and identify which assumptions have the greatest influence on the outcome.  
  调整预算、留存和付费假设，比较不同情景下的预估现金流与回本周期，识别对测算结果影响最大的变量。

### PlayBridge

PlayBridge covers two sides of browser gaming. Ludo focuses on real-time play, and PlayBridge Platform gives teams tools to distribute games and web apps and measure campaign performance.

PlayBridge 围绕浏览器游戏的体验与分发展开。Ludo 聚焦实时多人玩法，PlayBridge 程序则为游戏和 Web 应用团队提供分发与投放分析工具。

#### Ludo｜多人飞行棋游戏

Ludo adapts the classic board game for real-time multiplayer play in the browser and can be installed as a progressive web app (PWA). Players can join a quick match, create a private room, face computer opponents at three difficulty levels, or take turns on a shared device.

Ludo 是一款以经典飞行棋为基础的实时多人 Web 游戏，支持在浏览器中直接游玩，也可作为渐进式 Web 应用（PWA）安装。玩家可以快速匹配、创建私人房间、挑战三档难度的电脑对手，或在同一台设备上轮流对弈。

- **POWER mode｜道具与技能模式**  
  Collect items on the board and use special abilities to open up new tactical choices during a match.  
  玩家可在棋盘上收集道具，并在对局中使用特殊技能，拓展战术选择。
- **Player and match operations｜玩家与对局运营**  
  Review player profiles and match history, and track gameplay activity through operational reports.  
  通过运营后台查看玩家资料与对局记录，并以报表追踪游戏活跃情况。

#### PlayBridge Platform｜PlayBridge 程序

PlayBridge Platform is a distribution and campaign measurement workspace for game and web app teams. It brings PWA installation page publishing, campaign link management, and attribution reporting into a single workflow.

PlayBridge 程序是面向游戏与 Web 应用团队的分发与投放分析平台。它将 PWA 安装页发布、投放链接管理和广告归因整合到同一工作流程，帮助团队管理安装入口并评估推广效果。

- **Installation page design and publishing｜安装页配置与发布**  
  Build PWA installation pages from templates, customize their copy and imagery, and preview the result before publishing.  
  基于模板配置 PWA 安装页，调整文案与视觉素材，并在发布前预览页面效果。
- **Campaign links and browser routing｜投放链接与访问路径**  
  Configure tracking links for ad campaigns and direct visitors to the appropriate installation entry point based on their browser.  
  为广告活动配置投放追踪链接，并按访客使用的浏览器引导至相应的安装入口。
- **Conversion funnels and registration attribution｜转化漏斗与注册归因**  
  Identify where users drop off between ad click and installation, and review registration attribution by tracking link or campaign.  
  定位从广告点击到应用安装各环节的流失，并按投放链接或广告系列查看注册来源。
